About Us

We are a nonprofit organization funding English literacy opportunities and business support for immigrants. Our goal is to increase English literacy for young adults and students across Nebraska, and to help immigrants start their own businesses.

How We Help

Two core programs, one mission: helping immigrants thrive in Nebraska.

English Literacy

Free and low-cost classes for young adults and students, designed around real conversation and business communication skills. Many organizations in Nebraska partner with us to provide English literacy classes for immigrants and refugees

Business Support

Guidance, funding connections, and mentorship for immigrants starting or growing a business in Nebraska.
